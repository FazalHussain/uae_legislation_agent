# Phase 5 — Outreach Message Templates

## Core principles

**Personalisation is everything.** Every message must include:
- The business name
- One specific observation (their Instagram, their Google rating, their missing website)
- A specific number (revenue estimate, review count, competitor comparison)

Generic messages get ignored. Specific messages get replies.

**Channel tone:**
- Instagram DM → casual, curious, short
- WhatsApp → conversational but professional, slightly longer
- Email → professional, structured, with a subject line that earns the open

**Never start with:** "I offer web design services", "Are you looking for a website?", "I can help you grow your business"

**Always lead with:** an observation, a compliment, a curiosity hook, or a specific problem they have.

## Instagram DM Templates

Character limit: aim for 250–320 characters (fits the preview without expanding).

### Template IG-1 — No website (most common)
```
Hey [Name]! Love your [food/cuts/vibe] — found you on Google.
Noticed you don't have a website yet. Did you know [competitor]
down the road is getting 300+ searches/month because of theirs?
Made you a quick demo. Want to see it? 👋
```

### Template IG-2 — Inactive account
```
Hey [Name]! Came across your page — great work. 
Noticed you haven't posted since [Month]. Businesses like yours 
miss 30–40% of potential customers when they go quiet online.
I built a website demo for you — takes 30s to view. Interested?
```

### Template IG-3 — Low review count / no Google profile
```
Hey [Name]! Your [product/service] looks amazing 👏
Quick question — are you on Google Maps? I searched for 
"[niche] in [area]" and couldn't find you. That's customers 
going straight to competitors. Made something to fix it. Want to see?
```

### Template IG-4 — Compliment hook (highest open rate)
```
Your [dish/cuts/product] literally made me stop scrolling 😍 
Quick thing — I looked you up to show a friend and couldn't 
find a website. Built you a free demo. Mind if I send the link?
```

## WhatsApp Message Templates

Length: 120–200 words. Conversational, warm, not salesy.

### Template WA-1 — No website
```
Hi [Name], hope you're having a great day!

I came across [Business Name] on Google and really liked what 
I saw — [specific compliment based on reviews/photos].

I wanted to reach out because I noticed you don't have a website 
yet. I checked, and businesses like yours in [area] are missing 
roughly AED [low]–[high]/month from customers who search online 
and can't find them.

I've already built a quick website demo for [Business Name] — 
it took me about an hour and I think you'll like it.

Would you be open to viewing it? No commitment, just 30 seconds 
of your time, and if you like it we can talk next steps.

[Your Name]
```

### Template WA-2 — Bad website
```
Hi [Name]!

I came across [Business Name] while researching [niche] businesses 
in [area]. Your reviews are great — [X] stars and [N] happy customers!

I checked your website and noticed a few things that might be 
costing you customers: [problem 1], [problem 2], [problem 3].

I put together a quick redesign concept — it only takes 30 seconds 
to view and it's completely free.

Would you mind if I sent you the link?

Happy to jump on a quick call too if you'd prefer. Just let me know!

[Your Name]
```

Worth a quick look?

[Your Name]

## Personalisation checklist

Before sending any message, confirm these are filled in:
- [ ] Business name (not "your business")
- [ ] Specific compliment (a real observation, not generic)
- [ ] Specific problem (from the audit, not guessed)
- [ ] Revenue estimate (from the scoring model)
- [ ] Demo link (real URL, not placeholder)
- [ ] Your name and contact info

If any are missing, do not send — go back and collect the data.

---

## Volume and cadence recommendation

For a new market/niche:
1. Start with 5 HOT leads — hand-craft every message
2. After first replies/sales, semi-automate for WARM leads
3. Use batch templates only for MEDIUM leads

Instagram DM → wait 4 days → WhatsApp → wait 3 days → Email → wait 5 days → Follow-up email → wait 2 weeks → Re-engage

Optimal outreach window (UAE): Sunday–Thursday, 9am–11am or 7pm–9pm GST. Avoid Friday–Saturday for B2C businesses (they're busiest then).
