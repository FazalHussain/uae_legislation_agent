## Template A — Restaurant / Café / Food

**Pages:** Home, Menu, Gallery, About, Contact

**Hero section:** Full-width food photo, headline: "Taste [cuisine type] at the Heart of [area]", CTA: "View Menu" + "Reserve a Table"

**Must-have sections:**
- Menu with categories and prices (PDF embed or native)
- Instagram feed widget (if active)
- Delivery links (Talabat / Noon Food / Careem Now buttons)
- Google Maps embed
- Opening hours (Monday–Sunday with Friday prayer break if applicable)

**Design direction:** Warm earthy tones OR clean white with bold accent. Food photography is everything — flag to client that 10+ high-quality food photos are non-negotiable.

**v0.dev demo prompt:**
```
Create a mobile-first restaurant landing page for [Name], a [cuisine] restaurant in [area], Dubai.
Include: hero with full-width food image and "View Menu" CTA, menu section with 3 categories,
Instagram grid (6 placeholder images), delivery app badges (Talabat, Noon Food), Google Maps embed,
opening hours, and WhatsApp floating chat button. Colors: [warm amber + cream / deep green + gold].
Clean, appetite-stimulating design.
```

## Demo link platforms

After generating the v0.dev prompt, also mention these tools for instant demos:

- **v0.dev** — paste the prompt, get live React component in 60 seconds. Share the URL.
- **Framer.com** — AI site generator, full website from description. Best for polished demos.
- **Typedream.com** — fastest "link in bio" style pages. Good for restaurant/salon quick demos.
- **Durable.co** — AI website builder specifically for local businesses. Entire site in 30 seconds.

Suggest the most appropriate based on complexity:
- Simple (1-page lead capture) → Typedream or Durable
- Standard business site → v0.dev or Framer
- E-commerce or booking → Framer + Calendly embed
